Coming next · Journey

A whole world of little wins.

The upcoming Journey brings 500 untimed word-map challenges across 20 colorful worlds. Mix short and long words, follow each turn’s length band and crossing rule, and plan for a clockwise, reverse or half-turn.

Reach the word goal, then aim for both mastery counters: a letter total and a wordplay goal. Retry freely, reveal a helpful word, and keep your best stars. Calm and Timed free play offer room to wander.

Journey is in preparation and is not yet assigned in TestFlight. It uses an expanded, licensed English word list. The available Classic beta, 0.1.0 (2), uses the earlier sample dictionary and campaign rules. Check your assigned build before following the Journey instructions.
