Find a new way through.

The Journey has 1,000 untimed challenges across 40 worlds. Mix short and long words, connect at different ends, and follow clockwise, reverse and half-turns. Meet each level’s goals within its word budget.

A new Daily challenge asks for twelve different six- to nine-letter words. Take your time, plan your next connection, and replay for free. Earned progress and Keepsake looks stay yours.

Build 1.0.0 (4) is available to invited TestFlight testers. Earlier beta builds have different levels and rules. Check your installed version and the beta guide.
