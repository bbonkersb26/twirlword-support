Updated November 19, 2026

The public app and the separate ad test

The public App Store release, TwirlWord 1.0.2 (8), contains no advertising SDK, in-app ads, purchases or external gameplay analytics. It does not require a game account or operate a cloud-save service. Gameplay runs on your device and can be played offline after installation.

TwirlWord Ad Test is a separate internal diagnostic app that uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) with official test ads. It is not an update to the public app. The advertising information below covers this internal testing and the proposed optional ad feature. Adding advertising to the public app would require a separately reviewed app update; a website or consent-message change cannot add an SDK to an installed ad-free version.

Your game data

Word maps, checkpoints, scores, Journey progress, Daily attempts, earned looks and preferences are stored on your device. The ad test also stores bonus-look records locally. TwirlWord's ad integration does not send your words, word maps or progress records to Google as an advertising payload.

Internal diagnostics can record ad-service event names and timestamps locally and use a separate, labeled test save. Diagnostic files or screenshots supplied for troubleshooting may be shared with the developer. Please avoid including personal information that is not needed.

Local records remain until replaced by gameplay or removed with app data. Deleting the app or its data may remove progress and bonus looks. Device backups are managed through your device and operating-system settings, not a TwirlWord backup server.

Optional advertising

The proposed feature offers an optional Starlit Paper look after eligible, saved Journey results. Watching an ad is not required to play, retry, keep progress or retain independently earned rewards. A bonus is awarded only after the ad service reports that it was earned. This feature is not available in public version 1.0.2 (8).

In an ad-enabled build, consent updates and ad preparation may contact Google before you choose to watch. In the normal game flow this begins on eligible results screens; the separate diagnostic app can begin these steps on its test screen. Official test ads do not mean that no information leaves your device.

Google and advertising partners

Google's advertising software may process IP addresses and approximate location, device identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, crash information and performance information. These may be used for advertising, measurement, service reliability and fraud prevention. The information and permitted uses depend on the software, configuration, region and your choices.

Google's consent form may list additional advertising partners and their purposes. Review Manage options and the vendor information in that form for the choices it presents and the partners' privacy information. Depending on your choices and region, listed partners may receive or process information in connection with ad requests. A partner's presence in the form does not mean it receives every request.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps and sites. Providers apply their own privacy and retention policies. Removing local TwirlWord data does not itself delete information already held by them.

TwirlWord's own ad integration does not request device-location access or supply GPS coordinates. Approximate location can still be derived from an IP address. A consent-form description of precise location does not grant an operating-system location permission or show that TwirlWord has supplied such coordinates.

Advertising choices

Where a consent form is shown, you can review its choices before continuing. In the internal diagnostic app, use Privacy choices when available to review or change the choices managed by UMP. In the proposed normal game flow, after the app has checked consent on an eligible results screen during that session, Settings provides Ad privacy choices when UMP requires that entry point. Settings also provides Hide bonus offers to suppress offers. Hiding offers is separate from changing consent and does not erase information already sent. These ad controls are absent from the SDK-free public version.

Regional advertising consent and Apple's tracking permission are separate. The current internal test configuration does not request Apple's tracking permission or use an IDFA consent message. This does not mean that the advertising software handles no data. Any public ad update must describe its actual tracking behavior and request Apple's permission before tracking where required. Normal play and earned progress do not depend on granting advertising permission.

Apple and TestFlight

Apple may process App Store, device and diagnostic information under its own settings and policies. If you use TestFlight, Apple collects beta usage and crash information and shares feedback with the developer. Depending on how you joined, this can include tester and device details, comments and screenshots. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information. Avoid including sensitive details in feedback.

This Render website

The website's own code does not set cookies, run analytics, show ads or collect information through a form. Render processes website requests, which may contain an IP address and browser details. See Render's privacy policy. Visiting an external link is subject to that service's own terms.

Support and privacy requests

TwirlWord is published by Brian Baker. Email relativityrush@gmail.com for TwirlWord support or a privacy request. The developer and email providers receive your address, message and attachments so the developer can respond and investigate. Include only the information needed. You can request deletion of support correspondence at the same address.

Google, other advertising partners, Apple, Render and email providers apply their own retention practices to information they process; this page does not promise a shared retention period or that removing the app deletes their records. For Apple-managed information, visit Apple's Data and Privacy page.

Changes

This notice distinguishes the installed ad-free release from internal ad testing and a proposed future ad update. It will be revised when the publicly available version or its data practices change. Questions can be sent through our support page.
