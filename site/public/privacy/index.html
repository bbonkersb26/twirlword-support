Privacy information for the TwirlWord beta, the update in preparation, and this website.

Updated November 13, 2026

Game data on your device

TwirlWord stores word maps, run checkpoints, scores, campaign progress, daily attempts and results, earned cosmetic rewards, and preferences in local app storage. This lets you return to a saved puzzle and keep your best results. The current beta does not require a game account or provide a cloud-sync service.

Gameplay runs on the device and can be played offline after installation. This version contains no in-app advertising, purchases, external analytics integration or in-game tracking service. Operating-system and beta-distribution diagnostics are separate, as described below.

Local saves and removal

Your saves and settings remain in local storage until they are replaced by gameplay or removed with the app’s data. Deleting the app or its data may remove your progress. Device backups and any retained backup copies are managed by your operating system and its backup settings; TwirlWord does not operate a separate backup server.

TestFlight diagnostics and feedback

When you test through TestFlight, Apple collects beta crash logs and usage information and shares information with the developer. Depending on how you joined the beta, this can include your name, email address and device information. Feedback and screenshots you submit are also shared through Apple’s service. See Apple’s TestFlight privacy information for its collection and retention details.

Beta feedback and diagnostics help the developer investigate problems and improve TwirlWord. Please include only the details needed to explain an issue, and avoid sending sensitive information in screenshots or comments.

This website

These pages are hosted on Render. The site’s own code does not set cookies, run analytics scripts, display ads, or include a form that collects personal information. Hosting the pages still involves processing web requests, which can include an IP address and browser request information. See Render’s privacy policy for its handling of information.

Links to Apple or other services take you to those services. Their own privacy terms apply when you use them.

Support messages and privacy requests

Email relativityrush@gmail.com for TwirlWord support or questions about information you shared with the developer. Your email service and the support mailbox process the message, your email address and any attachments you choose to send. The developer uses those details to respond and investigate the issue; they are not collected by gameplay.

Include only what is needed to explain your request. You can ask for deletion of support correspondence using the same address. Beta testers can also use TestFlight feedback. Our support page explains both routes.

Apple also provides a Data and Privacy page for information managed by Apple. Platform requests and local device data are handled through the relevant platform or device settings.

Changes to this information

This page describes the current TwirlWord beta and the Journey and Daily update in preparation. If TwirlWord adds features that change how information is handled, this page will be updated to describe those changes.
