Playing TwirlWord

Current public release: TwirlWord 1.0.2 (8) is available on the App Store and contains no advertising SDK, in-app ads or purchases. Internal test builds are separate; follow the notes for the version assigned to you. Earlier saved journeys keep their original rules.

Build a word

Follow the goal above the board and use the on-screen letter keys to build your word at the highlighted connection. The shared board letter is part of your word. A valid completed word turns the board automatically.

Use Undo to remove a letter. In Journey, Pause → Goals shows the full plan. Follow the controls shown in your installed version; older builds may use different entry controls.

Does TwirlWord have ads?

The public 1.0.2 (8) app has no ads. TwirlWord Ad Test is a separate internal diagnostic app using Google advertising software and official test ads. Test ads can still involve network requests and data processing; they are not live advertising in the public app.

A proposed future update would offer an optional bonus look after eligible saved Journey results. Watching ads would not be required to play, retry or keep earned progress. It would require a separately reviewed app update.

Internal testers can use Privacy choices when offered on the diagnostic screen. The proposed normal game flow also has ad preferences in Settings. These controls are not present in the SDK-free public version. Read the privacy notice for Google and partner processing, consent choices and the difference between hiding an offer and changing consent.

Why isn’t my word accepted?

TwirlWord uses a curated English word list. A real word can still be absent from the list. Your word must fit the selected crossing, use the available rack letters, and follow the rules shown for your current mode.

A Journey level does not allow a word already used in that level, including its opening word. Daily requires twelve different six- to nine-letter answers and excludes directly related inflections already used in that puzzle. Free play retains a recent-word restriction. An incorrect word does not spend a move. Use the entry controls to correct the pattern or try another word. A timed free-play clock continues while you think.

How does the Journey progress?

The Journey has 1,000 untimed levels across 40 worlds. Complete every displayed goal within the word budget to finish a level and unlock the next. Some levels ask you to follow a sequence; others combine word properties, lengths and choices of ends. Legal side moves can help set up a later goal.

Retry starts the same level again for free. It does not send you back to level one or remove earned stars and Keepsakes. This build has no answer-reveal buttons or advertising requirements. Confirming a restart replaces the current attempt.

Earlier saved journeys keep their own rules and progress. The earlier 1.0.0 (3) beta has 500 levels: reaching its word count completes a level, and the extra goals affect stars. Classic 0.1.0 builds use an earlier dictionary and campaign.

What does the Daily challenge include?

Every new Daily puzzle asks for twelve distinct six- to nine-letter words, with no timer. The date changes at midnight UTC. You can finish an unfinished attempt after that boundary, and replay a completed puzzle for free. The first result is kept separately from replay results.

This build includes a finite schedule of daily puzzles. If that schedule runs out, the app explains that a content update is needed; it does not silently recycle an old puzzle. Older saved daily versions remain labeled and retain their original rules.

Can I pause or play without a timer?

Use Pause to take a break. Journey, Daily and Calm free play are untimed. Timed free play has a turn clock; animation and saved pauses do not spend that clock. Reduced motion keeps the same rules and results.

What if a save or progress check needs attention?

Give the app time to finish checking your saved journey. If a Retry option appears, use it after the device has enough free storage and the app can access its files.

Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app as a first troubleshooting step: progress is stored locally. If the problem continues, email support with the message, your app version and any change you noticed after reopening. Internal testers can also use TestFlight feedback where available.

Does progress move between devices?

TwirlWord saves progress and settings on the device. The public app has no game account or cloud-sync feature. Device backup and restore behavior is managed by your operating system.

Why can’t I install or open the beta?

TestFlight access depends on your invitation and the builds available to your tester group. Check TwirlWord in TestFlight for the assigned version and its availability. Apple’s installation guide covers invitations, compatible devices and expired beta builds.

Internal builds can differ from the public app; check your assigned version and its test notes. See privacy information for local saves, separate ad testing and TestFlight feedback.
