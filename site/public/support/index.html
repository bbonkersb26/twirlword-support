Playing TwirlWord

Check your build: Classic beta 0.1.0 (2) is available to invited TestFlight testers. The Journey update described below is in preparation and is not yet assigned in TestFlight. Your assigned version determines which rules apply.

How do I make a word?

Pick an offered crossing letter on the board. Set the length and crossing position, then use the rack tiles to fill the other letters. Submit a valid word to turn the map. Classic uses quarter-turns. In Journey, follow the displayed amount and direction: clockwise or reverse quarter-turns, or a half-turn.

Your word must cross the existing map exactly once. New tiles cannot overlap other tiles or touch them along an edge away from the crossing. The letter you cross is already on the board; the other letters come from your eight-tile rack.

Why isn’t my word accepted?

The available Classic beta uses a limited sample English dictionary. The upcoming Journey uses an expanded, licensed English list; a word can still be absent from that list. Every word must fit the crossing and use the available rack letters.

In Journey, your word must fit the current length band. An inside-crossing task requires the shared letter to be inside your new word, not at either end. Do not repeat a word already used anywhere in the level, including its opening word. Classic and free-play runs retain their own recent-word restrictions. The game explains rejected placements without using up your tiles. In a timed Classic or free-play run, the clock continues while you correct a word.

How will Journey levels and stars work?

All 500 Journey levels are untimed. Reach the displayed word goal to finish a level and unlock the next. Each turn shows its length band, crossing task, and the turn’s direction and amount; short and long words both have a place.

Finish without Reveal for two stars. For three, finish unassisted and reach both mastery counters: the required letter total and the level’s wordplay goal. Free Reveal marks the run as Assisted, so a completed run earns one star. Retries are free and cannot lower your saved best stars.

These are upcoming Journey rules. In the available Classic beta, the first 25 campaign levels are untimed, later levels have timers, and the third star uses a score target. Existing Classic saves and earned stars are preserved when Journey is added.

What counts toward a Journey mastery goal?

The level shows one wordplay goal alongside its letter-total counter:

  • Length Palette: use the specified word lengths.
  • Inside Route: choose inside crossings on turns that do not already require them. Mandatory inside-crossing turns do not add to this optional-goal counter.
  • Letter Trail: cross through different letters.

The two counters describe different goals. A high letter total alone does not earn the third star.

Can I pause or play without a timer?

Use Pause to take a break. All upcoming Journey levels and Calm free play are untimed. The available Classic campaign is untimed for its first 25 levels and timed afterward. In a timed Classic run, opening Reveal does not stop or refill the clock.

What if a save or progress check needs attention?

Give the app time to finish checking your saved journey. If a Retry option appears, use it after the device has enough free storage and the app can access its files.

Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app as a first troubleshooting step: progress is stored locally. If the problem continues, email support with the message you see or use TestFlight feedback.

Does progress move between devices?

TwirlWord saves progress and settings on the device. The current beta has no game account or cloud-sync feature. Device backup and restore behavior is managed by your operating system.

Why can’t I install or open the beta?

TestFlight access depends on your invitation and the builds available to your tester group. Check TwirlWord in TestFlight for the assigned version and its availability. Apple’s installation guide covers invitations, compatible devices and expired beta builds.

The beta is still being developed. Dictionaries, features and device support can change between builds; check your assigned version and its test notes. See privacy information for local saves and TestFlight feedback.
