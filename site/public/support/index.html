Playing TwirlWord

How do I make a word?

Pick an offered crossing letter on the board. Set the length and crossing position, then use the rack tiles to fill the other letters. Submit a valid word to turn the map a quarter-turn.

Your word must cross the existing map exactly once. New tiles cannot overlap other tiles or touch them along an edge away from the crossing. The letter you cross is already on the board; the other letters come from your eight-tile rack.

Why isn’t my word accepted?

The beta uses a limited sample English dictionary, so some familiar words are not included. A word must also fit the crossing, use the available rack letters, meet the level’s minimum length, and avoid repeating one of your ten most recent words.

The game explains invalid placements without using up your tiles. In a timed turn, the clock continues while you think or correct a word.

How do the campaign and stars work?

In the campaign update, reach the displayed word goal to complete a level and unlock the next. The first 25 levels have no timer. Later levels gradually ask for more words, longer words and faster turns.

A completed unassisted run earns two stars, with a third for reaching the displayed score target. Free Reveal marks a completed run as Assisted and earns one star. Replaying cannot lower your saved best stars.

Availability of the campaign depends on your assigned beta build.

Can I pause or play without a timer?

Use Pause to take a break. Calm free play has no timer, and the campaign’s first 25 levels are untimed. On timed campaign levels, opening Reveal does not stop or refill the clock.

What if a save or progress check needs attention?

Give the app time to finish checking your saved journey. If a Retry option appears, use it after the device has enough free storage and the app can access its files.

Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app as a first troubleshooting step: progress is stored locally. If the problem continues, send the message you see through TestFlight feedback.

Does progress move between devices?

TwirlWord saves progress and settings on the device. The current beta has no game account or cloud-sync feature. Device backup and restore behavior is managed by your operating system.

Why can’t I install or open the beta?

TestFlight access depends on your invitation and the builds available to your tester group. Check TwirlWord in TestFlight for the assigned version and its availability. Apple’s installation guide covers invitations, compatible devices and expired beta builds.

The beta is still being developed. Its sample dictionary, features and device support can change between builds. See privacy information for local saves and TestFlight feedback.
