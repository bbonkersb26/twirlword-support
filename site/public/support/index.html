Playing TwirlWord

Check your build: Version 1.0.0 (5) is in App Store review. Build 6 is for internal verification and has not replaced that submission. Check TestFlight for the builds assigned to you. Earlier saved journeys keep their original rules.

Build 5: tap controls

Choose a crossing with End A or End B and set your word length. Tap rack letters to fill the word, then tap Turn to play it. Follow the goal above the board.

These are the controls in the build currently under App Store review.

Build 6: swipe controls (internal testing)

Tap a glowing letter on the board to choose your connection. Swipe through the rack letters, then lift your finger to play. A valid word turns the board automatically.

In the 1,000-level Journey and new Daily puzzles, the selected board letter is included at the beginning or end of your word. In Free play, include the highlighted borrowed letter in your swipe where you want the crossing.

Follow the goal at the top. In Journey, Pause → Goals shows the full plan. In Journey and Daily, Pause → Map lets you inspect your words.

With VoiceOver, activate letters individually, then activate the current word to play it. Clear starts a fresh spelling. A hardware keyboard also supports full-word entry and Enter.

Why isn’t my word accepted?

TwirlWord uses a curated English word list. A real word can still be absent from the list. Your word must fit the selected crossing, use the available rack letters, and follow the rules shown for your current mode.

A Journey level does not allow a word already used in that level, including its opening word. Daily requires twelve different six- to nine-letter answers and excludes directly related inflections already used in that puzzle. Free play retains a recent-word restriction. An incorrect word does not spend a move. In build 6, the pattern stays on screen in red with “Incorrect”; start another swipe to try again. A timed free-play clock continues while you think.

How does the Journey progress?

The Journey has 1,000 untimed levels across 40 worlds. Complete every displayed goal within the word budget to finish a level and unlock the next. Some levels ask you to follow a sequence; others combine word properties, lengths and choices of ends. Legal side moves can help set up a later goal.

Retry starts the same level again for free. It does not send you back to level one or remove earned stars and Keepsakes. This build has no answer-reveal buttons or advertising requirements. Confirming a restart replaces the current attempt.

Earlier saved journeys keep their own rules and progress. The earlier 1.0.0 (3) beta has 500 levels: reaching its word count completes a level, and the extra goals affect stars. Classic 0.1.0 builds use an earlier dictionary and campaign.

What does the Daily challenge include?

Every new Daily puzzle asks for twelve distinct six- to nine-letter words, with no timer. The date changes at midnight UTC. You can finish an unfinished attempt after that boundary, and replay a completed puzzle for free. The first result is kept separately from replay results.

This build includes a finite schedule of daily puzzles. If that schedule runs out, the app explains that a content update is needed; it does not silently recycle an old puzzle. Older saved daily versions remain labeled and retain their original rules.

Can I pause or play without a timer?

Use Pause to take a break. Journey, Daily and Calm free play are untimed. Timed free play has a turn clock; animation and saved pauses do not spend that clock. Reduced motion keeps the same rules and results.

What if a save or progress check needs attention?

Build 6 internal testers: Please report any change to your Free game after closing and reopening the app. This check remains under investigation.

Give the app time to finish checking your saved journey. If a Retry option appears, use it after the device has enough free storage and the app can access its files.

Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app as a first troubleshooting step: progress is stored locally. If the problem continues, email support with the message you see or use TestFlight feedback.

Does progress move between devices?

TwirlWord saves progress and settings on the device. The current beta has no game account or cloud-sync feature. Device backup and restore behavior is managed by your operating system.

Why can’t I install or open the beta?

TestFlight access depends on your invitation and the builds available to your tester group. Check TwirlWord in TestFlight for the assigned version and its availability. Apple’s installation guide covers invitations, compatible devices and expired beta builds.

The beta is still being developed. Dictionaries, features and device support can change between builds; check your assigned version and its test notes. See privacy information for local saves and TestFlight feedback.
