Playing TwirlWord

Check your build: The next update, with 1,000 Journey levels and a new Daily challenge, is in testing and has not been uploaded to TestFlight. The guide below distinguishes those new rules from the earlier beta. Your installed version and saved journey determine which rules apply.

How will the new Journey controls work?

Choose an available End A or End B, choose a word length, then fill the other letters from the rack. The crossing letter is already on the board. Press Turn it to play a valid word and animate the turn.

Check the displayed turn direction and the next available ends before committing. Your new word shares exactly one crossing with the map. Its other tiles cannot overlap existing tiles or touch them along an edge away from that crossing.

The Plan view explains the level goals. The word map and reading strip keep longer words readable. When a full draft cannot fit on the map, the map shows its labeled host word and the complete draft remains below it.

Why isn’t my word accepted?

TwirlWord uses a curated English word list. A real word can still be absent from the list. Your word must fit the selected crossing, use the available rack letters, and follow the rules shown for your current mode.

A Journey level does not allow a word already used in that level, including its opening word. Daily requires twelve different six- to nine-letter answers and excludes directly related inflections already used in that puzzle. Free play retains a recent-word restriction. Invalid input does not spend a move; a timed free-play clock continues while you correct it.

How will the new Journey progress?

The next Journey has 1,000 untimed levels across 40 worlds. Complete every displayed goal within the word budget to finish a level and unlock the next. Some levels ask you to follow a sequence; others combine word properties, lengths and choices of ends. Legal side moves can help set up a later goal.

Restart level starts the same level again for free. It does not send you back to level one or remove earned stars and Keepsakes. The new update has no answer-reveal buttons or advertising requirements. Confirming a restart replaces the current attempt.

Earlier saved journeys keep their own rules and progress. The earlier 1.0.0 (3) beta has 500 levels: reaching its word count completes a level, and the extra goals affect stars. Classic 0.1.0 builds use an earlier dictionary and campaign.

What will the Daily challenge include?

Every new Daily puzzle asks for twelve distinct six- to nine-letter words, with no timer. The date changes at midnight UTC. You can finish an unfinished attempt after that boundary, and replay a completed puzzle for free. The first result is kept separately from replay results.

The update includes a finite schedule of daily puzzles. If that schedule runs out, the app explains that a content update is needed; it does not silently recycle an old puzzle. Older saved daily versions remain labeled and retain their original rules.

Can I pause or play without a timer?

Use Pause to take a break. The next Journey, Daily and Calm free play are untimed. Timed free play has a turn clock; animation and saved pauses do not spend that clock. Reduced motion keeps the same rules and results.

What if a save or progress check needs attention?

Give the app time to finish checking your saved journey. If a Retry option appears, use it after the device has enough free storage and the app can access its files.

Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app as a first troubleshooting step: progress is stored locally. If the problem continues, email support with the message you see or use TestFlight feedback.

Does progress move between devices?

TwirlWord saves progress and settings on the device. The current beta has no game account or cloud-sync feature. Device backup and restore behavior is managed by your operating system.

Why can’t I install or open the beta?

TestFlight access depends on your invitation and the builds available to your tester group. Check TwirlWord in TestFlight for the assigned version and its availability. Apple’s installation guide covers invitations, compatible devices and expired beta builds.

The beta is still being developed. Dictionaries, features and device support can change between builds; check your assigned version and its test notes. See privacy information for local saves and TestFlight feedback.
